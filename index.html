The board always has all 4 colors in play. Choose how many people are at this device — with fewer than 4, a player can control more than one color, just like team Ludo.

What's happened
    • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto the board.
    • You have two dice. You can use the numbers on both dice during your turn.
    • A 6 can bring a new token out, while the other die can be used to move a token.
    • You can use both dice on the same token or on different tokens when possible.
    • Land exactly on an opponent's token to send it back to its yard — unless that square is a safe spot.
    • Two tokens of the same color on one square form a block that opponents cannot land on.
    • Rolling a 6 gives you another roll after completing the current dice.
    • Move all four of your tokens home to win.
