    • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto the board.
    • Move a token forward the number shown on the dice.
    • Land exactly on an opponent's token to send it back to its yard — unless that square has a star, which is a safe spot.
    • Two of your own tokens sharing a square form a block opponents can't land on.
    • Rolling a 6 earns another roll. Get all 4 tokens home to win.
